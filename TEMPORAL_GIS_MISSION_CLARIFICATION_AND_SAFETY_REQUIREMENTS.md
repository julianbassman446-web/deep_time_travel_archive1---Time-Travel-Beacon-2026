PERSONAL LIFE PLAN CORRECTION: GROWTH, FRIENDSHIP, INDEPENDENCE, AND BALANCE

Purpose: To record the values, lessons, relationships, and life experiences I want to carry forward.

1. My Identity and My Future

I want to remain myself: imaginative, curious, helpful, expressive, and interested in the world around me. Having a disability does not mean that I should be denied independence, friendship, respect, or the opportunity to make my own decisions.

I want a happy, ordinary, fulfilling life that includes family, friends, college, hobbies, movies, television, adventures, social events, and new experiences. I want to pursue my goals while learning to balance my wishes with other people's needs and boundaries.

I do not have to become a different person to grow. I can learn new skills, accept feedback, and change my behavior when needed without giving up the parts of myself that I value.

2. My Known List and My Relationships

My Known List consists of the people in my life and the relationships I want to remember, appreciate, and develop appropriately.

I want this list to grow naturally over time. I want to spend time with people I enjoy being around while remaining open to meeting others. I also recognize that everyone has their own friends, interests, responsibilities, preferences, and personal boundaries.

I want to:

Invite people to activities without pressuring them.

Ask for contact information or make plans in moderation.

Accept a person's answer, including a refusal or rejection.

Respect requests for distance and any no-contact restrictions.

Allow friendships to change, grow, or end.

Avoid yelling, overreacting, or assuming that disagreement means someone does not care about me.

Recognize that other people can care about me while still disagreeing with me.

Offer advice when appropriate without trying to control another person's choices.

Friendship is mutual. I have a right to make choices about my relationships, and other people have that same right.

3. Learning From Mistakes

I want to use past experiences to make better decisions in the future. I can acknowledge mistakes, understand their effects, apologize when appropriate, and develop healthier habits.

Before acting, I can ask myself:

Could this hurt someone or cross a boundary?

Am I respecting the other person's answer?

Am I acting impulsively or reacting to disappointment?

Is there a calmer or kinder way to approach this?

Would asking a trusted person for advice help?

Am I accepting the possibility that the answer may not be the one I want?

I do not need to punish myself for past mistakes. I need to learn from them and make responsible choices now.

Television shows and movies can inspire me, but fictional characters do not always model healthy relationships. I can enjoy imaginative stories while choosing my own behavior according to real-life consent, safety, and respect.

4. Independence, Disability, and Accepting Help

I want people to take my perspective seriously and respect my ability to make decisions. I also want to recognize that listening to advice, accepting reasonable limits, and asking for help can be strengths rather than weaknesses.

I can advocate for myself without assuming that everyone who disagrees with me is trying to manipulate me. Likewise, I can recognize when someone has treated me unfairly and explain my concerns calmly.

When I need help, I can ask for it. When other people offer useful guidance, I can consider it without feeling that I must abandon my identity or accept every opinion automatically.

I can work toward independence while still benefiting from support, cooperation, and mutual understanding.

5. Communication With My Known List

I want to explain my needs in a respectful and comfortable way. I want people to understand that my imaginative approach can be meaningful and helpful, while I also remain willing to listen and learn.

I can say that I want to be treated as an equal, have my preferences considered, and be allowed to make age-appropriate decisions. I can also acknowledge that other people have their own needs and do not always have to agree with me.

I will aim to communicate honestly without demanding that others respond in a particular way. I can express disappointment, ask questions, and explain my perspective while accepting that another person may need time, distance, or a different solution.

The goal is not to win every disagreement. It is to understand each other better and decide what is healthy for everyone involved.

6. Everyday Life and Meaningful Experiences

I want to make room for ordinary life alongside my larger ambitions. That includes:

Spending time with friends and family.

Attending appropriate social events, birthday parties, and outings.

Exploring college and educational opportunities.

Enjoying movies, television, music, and hobbies.

Sharing adventures and updates when I choose to do so.

Meeting new people and making introductions when everyone is comfortable.

Pursuing creative projects and contributing to my community when opportunities are available.

I want to participate in life rather than spend all my time reviewing the past or trying to make every experience happen in exactly the same way.

I can preserve the memories that matter while making new ones.

7. Boundaries, Rejection, and Emotional Balance

I want to learn that rejection is a normal part of life. I can feel disappointed without treating the experience as a disaster or assuming that my future is ruined.

I can decide that a relationship is not right for me, just as another person can decide that a relationship is not right for them. I can respect their choice and still value myself.

I want to notice how my actions affect other people, take responsibility when needed, and find a balance between advocating for myself and respecting others.

I do not need to sacrifice every personal goal to keep everyone happy. I can make thoughtful decisions about which goals to pursue, which compromises are reasonable, and which boundaries must be respected.

8. My Hypothetical Alternate-Timeline Idea

If I continue exploring a hypothetical life redo or backward-time-travel scenario, this plan represents the values and experiences I would hope to carry forward.

However, I recognize that backward human time travel has not been demonstrated. My notes, memories, simulations, and requests cannot guarantee a trip into the past or ensure that an alternate timeline would unfold as intended.

I can still put these lessons into practice in the present. I do not need a time-travel system to begin building healthier relationships, pursuing my goals, or making meaningful memories.

9. My Continuing Commitment

I will try to learn from mistakes without defining myself by them. I will respect other people's choices while advocating for my own. I will remain imaginative and curious while making decisions with care.

I will make room for friendship, family, education, creativity, independence, and ordinary happiness. I will seek help when it is useful, offer help without imposing it, and allow relationships to develop naturally.

My goal is not to recreate every detail of the past. It is to build a meaningful life, learn as I go, and move forward as myself.
